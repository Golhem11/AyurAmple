AyurAmple Showcase Website
==========================

Files:
- index.html          Home page
- products.html       Product showcase + category filters
- about.html          Brand story / positioning
- bulk-supply.html    B2B & B2C bulk supply page
- contact.html        Contact details + enquiry form
- styles.css          Responsive styling
- script.js           Product filters + enquiry mailto handler
- assets/             Brand poster and cropped product images

How to use:
1. Unzip the folder.
2. Open index.html in a browser.
3. For a live website, upload the folder contents to your hosting/server.

Notes:
- The site is a static showcase and needs no backend.
- The enquiry form opens the visitor's email client.
- Product visuals are cropped from the supplied AyurAmple promotional artwork.
- Phone, email and address shown on the site are taken from the supplied artwork.
